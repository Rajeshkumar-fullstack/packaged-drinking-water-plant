Data Protection & Confidentiality

Privacy Policy.‎

Learn how AquaPure Automated Drinking Water Plant collects, utilizes, and safeguards your customer, ordering, and institutional account information.

Last Updated: November 2026 • Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Compliant

1. Commitment to Privacy

AquaPure Packaged Drinking Water Plant ("AquaPure", "we", "our", or "us") operates automated water purification and bottling lines supplying household, retail, and commercial bulk water. We respect your fundamental right to privacy and are dedicated to processing personal data transparently, securely, and strictly for legitimate commercial fulfillment purposes.

2. Information We Collect

When you place an order, request a corporate quote, or register for water can distribution, we may collect the following operational data points:

  • Contact Particulars: Full name, verified mobile phone number, delivery address, and email coordinates.
  • Order & Product Records: Volume requested (20L cans, 10L cans, PET bottle cartons, water pouches), delivery frequency, and payment preferences.
  • Institutional Billing: Corporate entity legal name, GST identification number, and accounts payable contact for monthly ledger invoices.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Browser user-agent, preferred theme mode (Light/Dark), and directional layout preferences stored locally on your device.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Your information is processed strictly to execute water supply logistics:

  • Dispatching fresh water supplies from our automated bottling facility directly to your delivery destination.
  • Generating valid GST tax invoices and returnable 20L can deposit reconciliations.
  • Delivering vital statutory notices, batch quality testing logs, and microbiological laboratory compliance certificates.
  • Responding directly to customer service queries and distributor territorial franchise inquiries.

4. Data Security & Retention

All digital communications and order submissions utilize high-grade 256-bit TLS encryption. Access to customer contact lists and delivery addresses is strictly restricted to certified dispatch controllers. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade customer contact records to third-party telemarketers or external brokers.

5. Cookies & Local Storage Policy

This website does not deploy behavioral advertising trackers. We employ standard browser localStorage purely to remember your shopping cart items, theme setting (Dark/Light Mode), and reading direction (LTR/RTL) across browser sessions. You may clear your browser cache at any time to remove these local preferences.
