Our Heritage

From Regional Bottler to Modern Water Processing Facility

AquaPure started with a single bottling line focused on supplying hygienic 20-litre cans to local businesses. Over the years, increasing urbanization and heightened awareness of water-borne contaminants prompted our strategic investment into a fully automated, high-throughput processing plant.

Today, our plant integrates European automated bottling machinery, enclosed Class-100 sterile filling rooms, high-pressure Reverse Osmosis systems, and an in-house chemical and microbiological testing laboratory that operates round-the-clock.

"Water purity is not just a commercial specification for us; it is a sacred public health responsibility that governs every valve we turn."

Engineering

State-of-the-Art Plant Infrastructure

A comprehensive look at our processing equipment, raw storage capacity, and cleanroom sanitization systems.

SS316 Stainless Steel Fluid Network

All treated water transit lines, buffer reservoirs, and dosing valves are constructed from electro-polished sanitary SS316 to eliminate bacterial biofilm formation.

Class-100 Enclosed Positive Air Capping

Positive-pressure laminar air flow cabinets purge airborne dust and bio-particulates away from container openings during rapid filling and mechanical hermetic sealing.
