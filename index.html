Who We Are

Committed to Health, Hygiene, and Uncompromised Purity

AquaPure operates one of the region's most modern automated packaged drinking water facilities. Designed with international hygiene principles, our plant takes raw spring and treated source water through rigorous physical, chemical, and microbiological purification.

From institutional corporate supplies and commercial water cans to retail bottled water for events and daily hydration, we maintain strict batch tracing and round-the-clock testing to ensure every drop satisfies your thirst safely.

Purification Journey

Multi-Stage Multi-Barrier Purification Process

Every drop goes through a scientific 8-stage purification cycle to eliminate physical impurities, microbial contaminants, and harmful pathogens while preserving beneficial trace elements.

01

Dual Media
Sand Filtration

Removes turbidity, coarse suspended particles, and heavy debris from raw intake water.

02

Activated Carbon
Block Adsorption

Eliminates chlorine, volatile organics, foul odors, and color pigments for fresh taste.

03

Micron Candle
Precision Polishing

Precision 5-micron and 1-micron dual sediment filters trap ultra-fine particulates.

04

Reverse Osmosis
TFC Membrane

Advanced TFC membranes eliminate dissolved salts, heavy metals, and toxic solutes.

05

Essential Mineral
Fortification Stage

Controlled infusion of Calcium and Magnesium restores vital minerals and balanced pH.

06

Ultraviolet Ray
Sterilization Chamber

High-intensity germicidal UV irradiation destroys bacterial DNA and active pathogens.

07

Medical Ozonation &
Disinfection

Medical-grade ozone infusion ensures continuous residual protection against microbes.

08

Class-100 Enclosed
Sterile Bottling

Cleanroom Class-100 positive‑pressure automatic rinse, cap cycle.

Our internal analytical and microbiological laboratory monitors each production stage. Every single batch is logged, tested against turbidity, residual ozone, mineral conductivity, and bacterial cultures before dispatch approval.

Infrastructure

Automated Bottling & Packaging Facility

Engineered with high-grade SS316 food contact piping, sterile HEPA air filtration zones, and computerized filling speeds exceeding 120 bottles per minute.

Class-100 Sterile Filling Cabinets

To maintain the highest hygiene standards, the capping and filling zones operate inside enclosed positive-pressure laminar air chambers. Filtered air removes any airborne particles while UV tunnels sterilize bottle preforms prior to liquid filling.

HEPA Filters

99.97% airborne particle exclusion in bottling rooms.

Clean-in-Place (CIP)

Automated steam & ozone cycle cleaning every 8 hours.
