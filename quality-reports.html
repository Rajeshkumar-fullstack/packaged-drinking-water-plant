Testing Protocols

Three-Tiered Analytical Testing Protocol

Tier 1: Real-Time In-Line Monitoring

Continuous automated probes measure conductivity, pH, and dissolved ozone before filling heads.

Tier 2: Hourly Bench Chemistry

Trained chemists perform manual titrations for hardness, alkalinity, and residual sterilants.

Tier 3: 48-Hour Microbial Incubation

Sample bottles from each batch undergo selective bacterial culturing before final dispatch release.

Plant Sanitization

Strict Manufacturing Hygiene Standards

Water purity requires total operational hygiene. Our plant adheres to a zero-compromise facility sanitization code designed around pharmaceutical cleanroom protocols:

  • Clean-In-Place (CIP) Cycles: Every 8 hours, automated caustic and hot ozone cycles flush all storage tanks and delivery pipes.
  • Laminar Positive Air Pressure: Packaging enclosures are kept at positive pressure to push ambient air out, preventing dust ingress.
  • Operator Sanitization Air Showers: Technicians pass through automated footwear sanitization, hairnets, and air showers before entering.

Frequently Asked Questions on Water Quality

Everything you need to know regarding TDS levels, mineral balance, shelf-life, and storage guidelines.

TDS represents the concentration of dissolved minerals such as Calcium, Magnesium, and Potassium. Over-demineralized water (below 30 PPM) tastes flat and lacks physiological hydration benefits. Very high TDS (above 300 PPM) feels heavy and salty. Our range of 80–120 PPM delivers optimal crisp taste and healthy daily mineral replenishment.
UV sterilization uses ultraviolet rays to neutralize bacteria and viruses, but it cannot remove heavy metals, dissolved toxic salts, or chemicals. Reverse Osmosis physically forces water through micro-pores (0.0001 microns) to strip dissolved impurities. AquaPure combines both RO and UV to ensure both chemical and biological purity.
Sealed PET bottles maintain their certified freshness for up to 6 months when stored in a cool, shaded environment away from direct sunlight. For 20L commercial cans, we recommend consumption within 21 days from manufacture to enjoy optimal taste and dissolved oxygen freshness.
