Cleanliness Guarantee

Automated 7-Stage Can Sanitization Line

Before any recycled can is refilled, it must pass an unbroken sequence of computerized cleaning cycles without human hands ever touching the mouth.

Stage 1: Pre-Inspection & De-capping

Visual and optoelectronic sniff inspection for foreign odors or cracks, followed by automatic cap removal.

Stage 2 & 3: High-Pressure Alkaline & Acid Wash

Dual-phase rotary internal jets dissolve organic films and mineral scale at 65°C.

Stage 4 & 5: RO Rinsing & Ozonated Sterilization

High-purity RO water rinses away wash agents; medical ozone water blast sterilizes internal walls.

Stage 6 & 7: Sterile Filling & Laser Capping

Filled inside enclosed Class-100 cleanroom chambers, capped with tamper-proof seal and batch coded.
