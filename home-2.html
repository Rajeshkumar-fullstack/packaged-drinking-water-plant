Scientific Workflow

Automated Industrial Process Architecture

An overview of the engineering controls that govern our raw water intake through de-salination, remineralization, and pharmaceutical-grade sterilization.

STEP 1

Turbidity & Media Coagulation

High-efficiency quartz sand and anthracite layers capture colloids Media Coagulation down to 20 microns.

STEP 2

Organics Dechlorination

Virgin coconut-shell carbon bed neutralizes VOCs, pesticides, and residual oxidation agents.

STEP 3

High-Pressure Brackish RO

Multi-stage polyamide spiral membranes operate at 15 bar pressure, separating ionic solutes.

STEP 4

Micro-Mineral Dosing

Proportional metering pumps restore ionic Ca2+ and Mg2+ to exact physiological equilibrium.

STEP 5

Ozone Retention Contact Tower

Diffused ozone generator maintains 0.2 to 0.4 ppm residual Ozone Retention sterilant for safe storage.

STEP 6

254nm UV Chamber

Sub-second germicidal irradiation breaks good qulity biological cell walls prior to bottling valves.

Robotic Packaging & Cleanroom Excellence

Our manufacturing floors integrate PLC-controlled rotary monobloc rinsing, filling, and capping machinery. Operating inside ISO Class 7 cleanrooms, bottles and cans are inverted, rinsed with ozonated pure water, filled to volumetric accuracy, and laser capped in under 4 seconds.

Packaging Engineering

Advanced PET Blow Moulding & Eco-Packaging

From preform expansion to high-strength pallet shrink wrapping, our packaging technology optimizes container strength and environmental circularity.

Rotary Stretch Blow Moulding

Bottles are blown directly on-site using food-grade virgin preforms, eliminating transit dust and ensuring sterile internal bottle walls.

Laser Batch Code Engraving

High-precision fiber laser markings print batch codes, manufacture timestamps, and expiry parameters directly on the bottle neck.

Heavy-Gauge Shrink Wrap

Automated bundling in tamper-evident corrugated cartons and heavy LDPE shrink film prevents scuffing, puncture, and moisture ingress.
