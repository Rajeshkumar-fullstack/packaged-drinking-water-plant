Commercial Agreements & Logistics

Terms of Supply.‎

Standard operating terms governing product specifications, 20L returnable container deposit protocols, delivery schedules, and institutional accounts.

Conforming to IS 14543:2018 • FSSAI License Regulated Operations

1. Product Scope & Statutory Compliance

All packaged drinking water supplied by AquaPure is produced in our automated manufacturing facility strictly conforming to Bureau of Indian Standards specifications (IS 14543:2018) and Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) licensing criteria. Products encompass:

  • 20L & 10L Water Cans: Virgin food-grade Polycarbonate / PET containers fitted with tamper-evident heat-shrink security caps.
  • Packaged PET Bottles: 250ml, 500ml, 1L, and 2L virgin polymer bottles packed in master corrugated cartons or shrink-wrapped bundles.
  • Hygienic Water Pouches: 200ml and 500ml 3-layer co-extruded food-grade pouches in master transport sacks.

2. Returnable 20L Can Container Protocol

To ensure environmental stewardship and cost-effective commercial hydration:

  • Security Deposit: A standard refundable deposit (₹150 per can) applies to all newly issued 20L polycarbonate dispenser containers.
  • One-for-One Exchange: Regular deliveries operate on a 1-for-1 empty return basis. Empty cans must be returned undamaged, internally unsoiled, and free of foreign liquids or chemical contaminants.
  • Sanitization & Re-fill: Returned cans undergo a 7-stage automated industrial washing protocol (high-pressure hot water, alkaline wash, and ozonated rinse) before refill.
  • Deposit Refund: When discontinuing supply, customers returning original issued cans in sound condition receive 100% refund of their security deposit within 3 working days.

3. Delivery Scheduling & Fleet Access

AquaPure maintains an automated dispatch fleet operating 6 days a week (Monday to Saturday):

  • Standard residential and office delivery orders confirmed before 11:00 AM are scheduled for same-day afternoon dispatch.
  • Institutional bulk orders (over 50 cans or 30 carton bundles) are scheduled on pre-assigned route cycles with dedicated plant route supervisors.
  • Recipients must provide safe vehicular offloading access or designated security receiving bay clearance.

4. Pricing, Invoicing & Payment Terms

All product prices are quoted in Indian Rupees (₹) inclusive of applicable GST:

  • Accepted Methods: Cash on Delivery (COD), Doorstep Instant UPI QR scan, and Online Bank NEFT/RTGS transfer.
  • Corporate Accounts: Pre-approved IT parks, universities, hospitals, and manufacturing factories are eligible for 15-day or 30-day monthly invoice payment cycles backed by institutional purchase orders.
  • Receipts: A digital tax invoice with batch code traceability is issued with every finished goods delivery.

5. Batch Inspection & Replacement Policy

AquaPure guarantees 100% seal integrity on delivery:

  • Customers are instructed to verify that the tamper-evident heat-shrink cap and batch QR hologram are intact upon doorstep handover.
  • In the rare event of in-transit container damage, compromised seals, or packaging leakages reported within 24 hours of delivery, our route team will replace the container free of charge during the next scheduled route stop.
